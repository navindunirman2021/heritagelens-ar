Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja

Ceremonial Tusker • Digital Interpretive Model

Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja was a celebrated ceremonial tusker associated with Sri Lanka’s Kandy Esala Perahera. This interactive model supports cultural learning by allowing visitors to observe the elephant from different viewpoints through augmented reality.

This is a digital interpretive model inspired by the elephant’s cultural significance. It is not presented as an exact scanned replica.
