Heritage Lion

Cultural Guardian • Interactive AR Interpretation

The lion is a familiar symbol in Sri Lankan cultural and artistic traditions, representing strength, guardianship, and heritage. Space View allows visitors to place this digital artefact in their own environment and inspect its shape, scale, and details.

This is a digital interpretive model intended for cultural learning. It is not presented as an exact replica of a specific historical sculpture.
