Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja

Ceremonial Tusker • Digital Interpretive Model

Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja was a renowned ceremonial tusker associated with Sri Lanka’s Kandy Esala Perahera. This AR exhibit supports cultural learning by allowing visitors to observe the elephant from different viewpoints.

This digital interpretive model is inspired by the elephant’s cultural significance and is not an exact scanned replica.
