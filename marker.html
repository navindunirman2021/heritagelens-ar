Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja

Ceremonial Tusker • Digital Interpretive Model

Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja was a renowned ceremonial tusker associated with Sri Lanka’s Kandy Esala Perahera. This interactive model is inspired by his cultural significance and allows visitors to explore the form and scale of a ceremonial elephant through augmented reality.

This is a digital interpretive model for cultural learning and is not presented as an exact scanned replica of Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja.

Cultural context Kandy Esala Perahera
Exhibit type Interactive AR interpretation
